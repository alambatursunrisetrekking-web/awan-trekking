Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking Private Tour

The Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking tour offers an exhilarating adventure combined with the serene beauty of a sunrise over Bali’s iconic volcano. This private tour includes hotel transfer and breakfast.

Reach the summit at 1,717 meters above sea level, enjoy views of the caldera lake and surrounding mountains, have breakfast at the summit, and return comfortably to your hotel.
