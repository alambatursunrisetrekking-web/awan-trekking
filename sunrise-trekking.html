Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking Private Tour

The Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking tour offers an exhilarating adventure combined with the serene beauty of a sunrise over Bali’s iconic volcano. This private tour includes hotel transfer and breakfast.

BOOK YOUR ADVENTURE

🌅 Tour Overview

Reach the summit at 1,717 meters above sea level, enjoy views of the caldera lake and surrounding mountains, have breakfast at the summit, and return comfortably to your hotel.

🌋 1,717 MDPL Mount Batur Summit
👥 Private Tour Local Professional Guide
🍳 Breakfast Breakfast at the Summit

➡️ Itinerary

1

Early Morning Pick-Up

Hotel pick-up typically starts around 1:30 AM–2:30 AM depending on your accommodation, ensuring you reach the summit in time for sunrise.

2

Trek to the Summit

The trek begins around 4:00 AM. The hike takes about 2 hours and is moderately challenging. Experienced local guides accompany participants.

3

Sunrise at the Summit

Enjoy sunrise from 1,717 meters above sea level, with views over the caldera lake, Mount Agung and, on clear days, Mount Rinjani on Lombok Island.

4

Breakfast

Breakfast is provided at the summit, including sandwich, eggs, fresh fruit, fruit juice, chocolate snacks and hot coffee, tea or chocolate.

5

Explore the Crater & Descent

Explore the main crater and take photos. The descent takes approximately 1.5–2 hours.

6

Return Transfer

Return to your hotel by private transfer, typically arriving around 10:00–11:00 AM.
